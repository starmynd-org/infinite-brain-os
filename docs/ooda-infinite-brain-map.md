# OODA and the Infinite Brain: the visual explainer

The artifact is `docs/ooda-infinite-brain-map.html`, a self-contained dark-space visual
explainer of how John Boyd's real OODA loop maps onto the Infinite Brain. Open it from disk
with a `file://` URL; it makes zero external requests.

## What it shows

- **Diagram 1, Boyd's real OODA web.** Observe, a dominant Orient with its five
  sub-elements, an optional Decide, and Act, wired by the seven concurrent pathways:
  feed-forward (blue), implicit guidance (green dashed, Orient to Act and Orient to
  Observe), and feedback (amber, the double loop). Hover a legend chip to isolate a
  pathway.
- **Diagram 2, the same web mapped to the brain.** Intake as Observe, the department as
  Orient (canon, rules, prior tasks, intake, head reasoning), the priority model as a thin
  Decide, workflows and swarms as Act. The feedback arrows are drawn red and dashed because
  they are the unbuilt half (the analytical plane is unowned).
- The three-meta-primitive diagnostic scorecard (orientation dominates: pass; interaction
  and isolation: weak; novelty: partial), the two-circuit model, and the feedback-plane
  gap.
- **The wager ledger section**: the ratified lifecycle (observation, routing, disposition,
  wager, verdict, improvement), the three new tables, the four scientific constraints,
  calibration as the headline metric, and the local-Postgres-default analytical plane. The
  ledger is a ratified design, not a running system.
