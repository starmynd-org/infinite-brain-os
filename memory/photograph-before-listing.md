# Memory: Photograph Before Listing

## What happened

The studio listed a new candle in early spring with a placeholder image, intending to add
photos within days. The listing sat live for two weeks and converted at near zero while
the same scent sold normally at the market stall.

## The lesson

Customers do not buy an unphotographed candle, whatever the description says. The listing
is not done until the photos are.

## The rule of thumb now

Photography is part of definition-of-done for every listing. The seasonal-collection
lifecycle reserves week six for it, and the weekly review flags any live listing without
true-to-color photos.
