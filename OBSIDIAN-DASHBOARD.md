## Graph view

The graph is colored by `lifecycle_state` (config ships in `.obsidian/graph.json`):
scratch, research, candidate, canon. Wikilinks and frontmatter edges are the lines. A
healthy brain grows denser around canon and keeps scratch at the edges.

## Habits

- New thought: capture it in `intake/sources/` or straight into a namespace as scratch.
- New lesson: `memory/`, with an edge to what it informs.
- End of a working session: the session closeout, then check the graph for orphans.
