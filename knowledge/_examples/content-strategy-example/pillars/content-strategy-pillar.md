## The pillar

The content program helps a specific audience see the problem they have, before it asks
them to consider a solution. Most content programs do this in reverse: they lead with the
product or the offer and then work backward to the problem. That reversal fails because
an audience that does not yet see the problem will not evaluate a solution seriously.

This pillar constrains the entire content program. Every angle, every piece, every
platform choice must serve one of the following outcomes:

- Name a problem the audience has but has not articulated yet.
- Show the gap between where the audience is and where they want to be.
- Make the cost of the unsolved problem visible in a specific, concrete way.

## What this pillar does not authorize

This pillar does not authorize feature-led content, comparison content, or product
updates as the primary content type. Those may exist at the bottom of the funnel as
supplementary material, but they are not the strategic content program.

This pillar does not authorize content that names the problem in a generic way. The
problem must be specific to the audience segment the namespace targets. Generic problem
framing is a sign the angle was not grounded in a real signal.

## How to test an angle against this pillar

Before building an angle, ask: does this angle help the audience see a problem they have,
or does it help the operator feel good about the product? If the honest answer is the
second, the angle is not aligned with this pillar.
