# About This Company

This is a pillar node. Pillar nodes capture foundational truths: who the company is,
what it does, who it serves, and what it stands for. They are the highest-confidence
nodes in the graph because everything else is grounded in them.

Replace this content with the real description of your company. The example below
shows the expected depth and structure.

---

## What the company does

Acme Analytics builds business intelligence tooling for mid-market e-commerce brands.
Our product ingests data from Shopify, Amazon, Meta, and Google, runs a normalised
attribution model, and surfaces weekly recommendations to operators and brand owners.

We are a 12-person company. The operator and three analysts serve clients directly.
Three engineers maintain the data pipeline and application. Two are in sales.

## Who we serve

Mid-market DTC brands with annual revenue between $2M and $50M. They typically have
a fractional CFO and no dedicated data team. They are making decisions from spreadsheets
and gut feel. We give them the same analytical infrastructure a large brand would build
internally, as a service.

## What we are not

We do not serve enterprise (above $50M). The integration complexity and custom
requirements exceed what our product supports. We do not serve B2B brands; our
attribution models are designed for DTC.

## Core belief

Good decisions require good information. Most mid-market brands have the data; they
lack the infrastructure and interpretation layer. We build the layer.

## How we work

Every client gets a weekly briefing: a structured interpretation of their numbers,
a diagnosis of what changed and why, and three recommended actions. Recommendations
are generated by AI and reviewed by an analyst before sending. We hold ourselves to
the standard that every recommendation is actionable within one week.

## Key relationships and context

- Our primary integration partners are Shopify (DTC channel) and Amazon (marketplace).
- Our data pipeline runs on Google Cloud Platform.
- Client data is processed in a per-client BigQuery project. No data crosses clients.
- We use Claude Code as our internal AI tooling layer.

## What changes often

Client-specific knowledge, analyst assignments, and product roadmap. These belong in
other nodes, not here. This pillar captures stable, company-level truth.
