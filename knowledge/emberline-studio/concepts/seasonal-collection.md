## Definition

A seasonal collection is the studio's release unit: four to six scents shipped together,
anchored by one returning scent customers already trust. New scents earn a place by fitting
the season's scene, not by novelty alone.

## The eight-week lifecycle

1. Weeks 1 to 2: brief. Pick the season's scene, the anchor, and the candidate scents.
2. Weeks 3 to 5: sampling and small-batch test pours, listings drafted in the brand voice.
3. Week 6: photography. A listing without photos does not launch (a lesson recorded in
   `memory/photograph-before-listing.md`).
4. Weeks 7 to 8: launch, then the first weekly review reads early orders from
   `data/orders-ledger.md`.

## Where it is applied

The filled example project at `projects/_example/PLAN.md` runs one full collection through
this lifecycle.
