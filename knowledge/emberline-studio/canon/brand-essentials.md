## The five essentials

1. **Who Emberline serves.** People furnishing small spaces who want one good candle, not a
   shelf of them. The repeat customer is the unit of the business.
2. **What it promises.** A candle that smells the way the listing said it would, burns the
   full stated hours, and arrives photographed true to color.
3. **Materials honesty.** Soy wax, cotton wicks, named fragrance houses. Every material is
   stated plainly on the listing; nothing is "proprietary blend".
4. **The sensory-specific voice.** Listings and emails describe one concrete scene per
   scent, never stacked adjectives. The voice rule operationalizes this.
5. **Small-batch cadence.** Four to six scents per seasonal collection, one returning
   anchor scent, nothing produced ahead of proven demand.

## Why this is canon

Every agent and workflow in the example set reads these essentials before acting: the
triage agent drafts replies in this voice, the description skill names these materials,
the weekly review judges decisions against the repeat-customer promise.
