## Findings

- **Anchor scent dominated.** Sea Salt and Bay outsold every other spring SKU 2 to 1.
  Every collection needs one anchor scent planned and stocked accordingly.
- **Photography lesson confirmed.** The three listings shot before publishing
  converted measurably better than the two shot after. The memory node holds; keep
  enforcing photograph-before-listing.
- **One stockout.** Rosemary Hearth sold out in week 3 and stayed dark for nine days.
  The reorder trigger fired too late; set the threshold at 30 percent of opening
  stock, not 10 percent.
- **Wholesale inquiries trending up.** Four unsolicited wholesale inquiries this
  collection versus one last collection. Worth a deliberate wholesale decision before
  autumn, not ad hoc replies.
- **Recommendation for autumn.** Build the collection around one anchor scent, cap
  the line at five SKUs, and shoot all photography before any listing goes live.
